# Privacy-Policy
No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect,store,or share any personal information.
Local Storage: All game progress and high scores are stored locally on your device only.
No Third-Party Tracking: We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools.
Offline Gameplay: The game is fully functional  offline and does not transmit any data.
Device Permissions: We only request necessary permissions for core game functionality.
Children's Privacy: Since we collect no data, the game is safe for players of all ages.
No Unique Identifiers: We do not access your device's IMEI, UUID, or any unique identifiers.
Security: Your data stays on your device, ensuring it remains under your control.
No Data Sharing: We never share or sell any data to third-party advertisers or services.
Transparency: This policy is straightforward because your privacy is our top priority.
